Expand the inspector terminal from the keyboard and fill it edge-to-edge.

- Press Cmd+Shift+J to toggle the terminal's zoom-expand (the same 2x enlargement as hovering it); while expanded, Left/Right arrows cycle across every tab — Setup, Run, and each terminal — and Escape collapses it.
- Hover-zoom now waits briefly before collapsing when the cursor leaves, so a quick overshoot no longer snaps the panel shut.
- The Setup, Run, and Terminal surfaces now fill flush to the panel edges instead of leaving an inset around the terminal.
